# ADR-001: Single Composition Flow for Field and Report Use

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-08-02

## Context

Exported maps serve two audiences at once: operational field delivery (fast, readable on a phone) and academic or extension reports (document-ready composition with institutional footer and locator insets). Separate layouts would double configuration cost and diverge from the existing export modal.

## Decision

Ship one export composition screen. The same preview, options, and PNG output serve both field sharing and document insertion. Do not introduce separate “Field” versus “Report” layout modes or dual export quality buttons in this PRD.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Two exit actions (document PNG vs light share PNG)

- **Description**: Same options screen with two export buttons at different resolutions.
- **Pros**: Optimizes file size for mobile sharing.
- **Cons**: Splits user attention; quality trade-offs become opaque.
- **Why rejected**: User chose a single flow for simplicity.

### Alternative 2: Two layout modes

- **Description**: User picks Relatório or Campo and defaults change.
- **Pros**: Tailored density per context.
- **Cons**: Extra mode switching; harder to keep one faithful preview.
- **Why rejected**: User chose one composition.

## Consequences

### Positive

- One mental model and one preview that matches the PNG.
- Paper size, orientation, and DPI remain available to tune output without a second workflow.

### Negative

- A single PNG may be larger than an idealized “share-light” asset.

### Risks

- Dense legends may be hard to read on small screens; mitigate with legend columns, font size, spacing, and position controls.

## Implementation Notes

Keep paper/orientation/DPI controls. Prefer sensible defaults that remain legible at typical phone and print sizes.
