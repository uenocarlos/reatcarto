# ADR-001: Open Account Registration and Verified Identity

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-07-31

## Context

Field workers need independent accounts for owning maps and recovering access without administrator intervention. The product must accept either an email address or username at login while ensuring that email-based recovery reaches the account owner.

## Decision

Allow open self-registration. Require full name, unique username, unique email address, password confirmation, organization, role or job title, phone number, and explicit acceptance of the current Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. Record the accepted document versions and acceptance time. Require email verification before a new user can create or edit maps. Allow login by either username or email, email-based password recovery, and self-service updates to profile fields, username, email, and password. A changed email must be verified again.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Administrator-created accounts

- **Description**: Only administrators provision field-worker accounts.
- **Pros**: Strong enrollment control and no unsolicited registrations.
- **Cons**: Creates an administrative bottleneck and conflicts with the requested registration screen.
- **Why rejected**: Users must be able to join independently.

### Alternative 2: Username-only accounts

- **Description**: Register and authenticate without a required email address.
- **Pros**: Fewer registration fields and no email verification step.
- **Cons**: No reliable self-service password recovery or identity confirmation.
- **Why rejected**: The user selected email plus username and email-based recovery.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Field workers can enroll and recover access without support.
- Verified email protects recovery and account notifications.
- Consent records support privacy accountability.

### Negative

- New users cannot create or edit maps until verification is complete.
- Changes to email or username require uniqueness checks and additional account-state handling.

### Risks

- Automated registrations may create abuse; apply reasonable anti-abuse controls without blocking legitimate workers.
- Verification delivery failures may block access; provide resend and clear status feedback.

## Implementation Notes

The TechSpec must define password policy, verification and recovery token lifetime, rate limits, email delivery, and safe uniqueness behavior without exposing whether unrelated accounts exist.
