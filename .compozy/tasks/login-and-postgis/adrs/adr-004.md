# ADR-004: Administrative Access with Auditing and Owner Notification

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-07-31

## Context

The product needs operational administration and public-content moderation. The user also selected administrator access to private maps, including edit and deletion, which creates elevated privacy and integrity risk.

## Decision

Provide two roles: field user and administrator. Administrators may list, activate, and deactivate accounts; hide or remove maps from the public gallery; and view, edit, or delete private maps for support and administration. Every administrator action affecting an account or map must create an immutable audit entry identifying the actor, action, target, time, and relevant before/after context. Notify the affected owner when an administrator changes account state, public visibility, or map data.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Account and public-content administration only

- **Description**: Administrators cannot access private maps.
- **Pros**: Stronger privacy and lower insider risk.
- **Cons**: Limits direct support and remediation.
- **Why rejected**: The user explicitly selected private viewing, editing, and deletion.

### Alternative 2: Untracked full administration

- **Description**: Permit broad access without audit or owner notification.
- **Pros**: Less product complexity.
- **Cons**: No accountability for sensitive interventions.
- **Why rejected**: The user selected both audit history and owner notification.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Administrators can resolve account and content problems.
- Audit and notification make privileged actions traceable.

### Negative

- Administrators hold broad access to private user data.
- Notifications and audit records add lifecycle and privacy obligations.

### Risks

- A compromised administrator account can affect all users; privileged access requires stronger safeguards.
- Notifications could reveal sensitive details; include enough context for accountability without unnecessarily reproducing private content.

## Implementation Notes

The TechSpec must define administrator provisioning, protection against self-escalation, audit retention and access, notification delivery, and safeguards for destructive actions.
